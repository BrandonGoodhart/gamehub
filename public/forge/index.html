    Getting it onto an iPad, iPhone or Mac
    1. Download the game saves one .html file. That file is the whole game.
    2. On iPhone or iPad it lands in Files › Downloads. Tap it — Safari opens it straight away.
    3. On a Mac, double-click it, or drag it onto Safari.
    4. No internet needed after that. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
    5. To share it with another teacher: AirDrop it, email it, or drop it in your class drive. Nothing to install.
